Overview
This repository contains a discounted cash flow (DCF) valuation of Reliance Industries Ltd, built from ten years of historical financials (FY17–FY26) and a five-year forecast (FY27E–FY31E). The model includes a WACC build-up, unlevered free cash flow, a terminal value, and a sensitivity table on discount rate and terminal growth. Figures are illustrative and should not be treated as investment advice.

Files

Reliance_Valuation_Model.xlsx: the Excel model, with four sheets: Financials (income statement, balance sheet and cash flow), Assumptions, WACC and DCF. Inputs are in blue and formulas are in black.
Reliance_Valuation_Summary.docx: a short write-up of the valuation result, key assumptions, sensitivity table and limitations.

Key assumptions

Sales growth: 9% a year for FY27E–FY29E, then 8%.
EBITDA margin: about 16%, close to the FY26 level.
Capex: 15% of sales. Depreciation: 5.8% of opening net block.
Tax rate: 22.4%. Dividend payout: 10%.
Cost of equity: 13.5% (risk-free rate 6.5%, equity risk premium 7%, beta 1.0). WACC: about 11.9%.
Terminal growth: 5%. Return on new invested capital in the terminal period: 12%.
Minority interests are not deducted, as the source data doesn’t report them separately.
